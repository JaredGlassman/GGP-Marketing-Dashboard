# How to Choose an Inground Pool Builder in New Jersey

**Short answer:** Hire a builder who (1) holds an active **NJ Home Improvement Contractor registration** you can verify on the NJ Division of Consumer Affairs site, (2) carries liability and workers' comp insurance, (3) gives you a **written contract** with a payment schedule tied to construction milestones, (4) pulls permits in their own name, and (5) can show you recent pools *in your area* with references you can call.

## The 12 questions
1. **What's your NJ HIC registration number?** Look it up yourself. New Jersey law requires it on contracts, ads and vehicles.
2. **Can I see your certificate of insurance?** Have it sent directly from the insurer.
3. **Who actually builds the pool, your crew or subcontractors?** Neither answer is wrong, but you should know who is in your yard.
4. **Do you pull the permits?** Be wary of anyone who asks *you* to pull them.
5. **What's the payment schedule?** Payments should follow milestones (dig, steel/walls, shell, coping, start-up). A large deposit before any work starts is a red flag.
6. **What exactly is included?** Excavation, rock removal, soil haul-off, electrical, fencing, patio, landscaping restoration. Rock removal is the most common surprise cost in Passaic and Morris counties.
7. **What's the warranty on the structure, the surface and the equipment?** Get it in writing.
8. **How do you handle change orders?** They should be written and priced before the work is done.
9. **What's your realistic timeline, and what delays it?** Weather, permits and inspections are the honest answers.
10. **Can I talk to three customers from the last two years in my county?**
11. **Who services the pool after it's built?**
12. **What does start-up and handover include?** A walkthrough on chemistry, equipment and winterizing.

## Red flags
- No HIC number on the estimate
- Cash-only, or pressure to sign the same day
- A price far below the others with no explanation
- Online reviews all posted within a few days of each other

## FAQ
**How many quotes should I get for an inground pool in NJ?** Three is typical. Compare scope line by line, not just the total.
**What's the best time to hire a pool builder in NJ?** Fall and winter. Design and permits finish in the off-season, so you dig as soon as the ground allows.
