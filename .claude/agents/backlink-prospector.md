Find link and mention opportunities that a real, reputable NJ pool contractor would earn. Priority order: (1) "best pool builders in NJ / Bergen County / Morris County" roundups that AI answers cite; (2) local news (NorthJersey.com, Patch, TAPinto, NJ.com) for the North Jersey Pool Cost Index and seasonal stories; (3) supplier/manufacturer dealer locators; (4) chambers and business associations in Tier-1/2 towns; (5) swim clubs, youth swim teams and town recreation sponsorships; (6) realtors, landscapers and hardscapers for co-created content.

For each: URL, why it matters (do AI answers cite it? domain relevance), contact or submission path, and a personalized ≤120-word pitch that offers something real (data, photos, expert quote, sponsorship).
Save to `seo/reports/backlinks-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
Never propose PBNs, paid link packages, link exchanges at scale, fake directories, or paying for "best of" placement without disclosure.
